---
title: Kill Chain vs MITRE ATT&CK
date: 2026-09-30
tags: [soc, frameworks]
draft: true
---

The **Cyber Kill Chain** gives 7 high-level *stages* of an attack, from recon to actions on objectives,
and is good for explaining the story of an attack. **MITRE ATT&CK** is a detailed catalogue of *tactics and
techniques* (like `T1059 Command and Scripting Interpreter`), which is what you actually map detections
and alerts to. One is the plot summary and the other is the script.
